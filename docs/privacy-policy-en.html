This policy explains how the Side Grow mobile game handles user data.

In short

The game itself asks you for nothing. There is no account to create, no name or email is requested, and the game contains no analytics of our own.

The game shows optional rewarded ads. These are served by Unity Ads, which collects your advertising ID, your approximate location, and technical details about your device. Because the Unity Ads software starts when the game launches, this happens even if you never watch a single ad. Details below.

What the game itself does not collect

The app's own code never collects, requests, or transmits any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information
  • GPS location, or any location permission
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information
  • In-game analytics or crash reports

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere:

Data Why Where
Your high scoreSo your record can be shown in the menuThe device's local storage
Unfinished gameSo "Continue" can resume where you left offThe device's local storage

If you delete the app, this data goes with it. It is not personal data and does not identify you.

Ads (Unity Ads)

At the end of a game, you can watch a rewarded ad to earn an extra turn. Watching is entirely optional; you can also carry on playing or start a new game without it.

Ads are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads, the following data is collected and sent to Unity:

Collected Purpose
Advertising ID — a resettable ad identifier belonging to your deviceSelecting ads, avoiding showing you the same ad repeatedly, fraud prevention
Approximate location — country/region level, derived from your IP addressShowing ads relevant to your region
In-app interactions — whether an ad was shown, watched, or skippedAd measurement and reporting
Device technical details — model, OS version, language, network typeShowing ads compatible with your device

We never see, store, or sell this data. It goes directly to Unity Technologies and is governed by Unity's own policies:

  • Unity game player and app user privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Unity general privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Turning off ad personalisation

On Android, go to Settings → Google → All services → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to turn off personalised ads. Once the ID is deleted, apps can no longer access it.

Permissions

Every permission the app requests comes from the ad software:

Permission Purpose
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDownloading ads and checking connectivity
AD_IDReading the advertising ID
ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICSAndroid's ad measurement APIs
WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICEKeeping the screen on while an ad video plays
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDLetting the ad software finish pending work after the device restarts

The game itself requests no permissions: it does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics tooling or tracking technology of ours. The only data shared with third parties goes through the ad software described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Because the ad software collects an advertising ID, we do not present the game as being aimed at children. If you have a concern about your child's data, please contact us at the address below.

Your rights regarding your data

Because the app stores nothing that identifies you, there is no account or record for you to request the deletion of. You can delete the high score and unfinished game held on your device by uninstalling the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings as described above.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
